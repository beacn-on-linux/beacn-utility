Pair Beacn Devices

Ensure that neither the Beacn App or regular Utility are running before proceeding!

Before the Utility can communicate with devices via your web browser, you must first grant it permissions (this is a good thing). Hit the button below and add the devices one at a time, they'll list underneath once pairing has been confirmed. Once paired, they should continue to work without further interactions.

To remove the permissions, simply click the info button in the address bar (on the left) and remove the permissions there.
